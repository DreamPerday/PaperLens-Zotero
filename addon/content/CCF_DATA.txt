PaperLens conference ranking data
=================================

Conference identity and CCF/CORE rankings are derived from CCFDDL:
https://github.com/ccfddl/ccf-deadlines

Source catalog: https://ccfddl.com/conference/allconf.json
CCFDDL is licensed under the MIT License.

Rankings belong to their respective systems. CCFDDL displays them; PaperLens
does not redefine those rankings. CCF, CORE and institutional ranking versions
may change over time. Saved rankings are labels on the Zotero item and should be
checked against the applicable official edition.
